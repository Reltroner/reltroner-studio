# 🌍 Asthortera vs Earth: Planetary Comparison

## A Planetary-Scale Reference for Reltroner Studio — Astralis Series

Asthortera is not an Earth-sized world with fictional countries placed on top of it.

It is a **planetary-scale civilization platform** whose enormous physical size, high land availability, multi-trillion sentient population, differentiated civilizational systems, interstellar migration history, and twelve-moon sky create a stage fundamentally larger than normal Earth-derived geographic intuition.

Earth is used in this document only as a **reader-facing external benchmark**. The comparison helps translate Asthortera's scale into familiar measurements.

This does **not** imply that Earth exists within Asthortera's narrative continuity. Earth-based countries, governments, corporations, landmarks, and geopolitical systems are not part of Asthortera canon.

The comparison therefore functions as a **meta-worldbuilding translation layer**:

```text
Earth-scale intuition
        ↓
Planetary comparison
        ↓
Asthortera-scale understanding
```

| Metric | Asthortera | Earth |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Planet Type** | Super Earth / Civilization World | Terrestrial Planet |

| **Surface Area** | **9,381,109,394 km²** | **510,072,000 km²** |
| **Volume** | **85,415,845,262,387 km³** | **1,083,206,916,846 km³** |
| **Water Surface** | **~52%** | **~71%** |

| **Surface Gravity** | **~11 m/s²** | **~9.8 m/s²** |
| **Sentient Population** | **~4.412 trillion** | External reference only; date-dependent |
| **Average SDI** | **0.938 / 1.000** | Not applicable |
| **Median Age** | **~252 years** | External reference only |
| **Life Expectancy** | **~475 years** | External reference only |
| **Urbanization Rate** | **~89.5%** | External reference only |

Asthortera's dimensions place it far outside ordinary Earth-scale intuition.

Its diameter is only several times larger than Earth's, but diameter alone is misleading. Surface area grows approximately with the **square of radius**, while volume grows approximately with the **cube of radius**.

As a result, a world that is roughly four times wider than Earth becomes vastly larger in total usable planetary space.

# 2. 📏 Derived Planetary Scale

## 2.1 Diameter Ratio

```text
54,646 km ÷ 12,742 km
≈ 4.29×
```

Asthortera is approximately **4.29 times Earth's diameter**.

This does not mean Asthortera contains merely four Earths worth of space. The difference compounds dramatically when surface area and volume are considered.

## 2.2 Surface-Area Ratio

```text
9,381,109,394 km² ÷ 510,072,000 km²
≈ 18.39×
```

Asthortera has approximately **18.39 times Earth's total surface area**.

A country that appears geographically modest on an Asthorteran map can therefore still be continental—or even multi-continental—when translated into Earth-scale intuition.

## 2.3 Volume Ratio

```text
85,415,845,262,387 km³ ÷ 1,083,206,916,846 km³
≈ 78.85×
```

Asthortera has approximately **78.85 times Earth's volume**.

> **Asthortera is not a visually enlarged Earth map. It is a genuinely gigantic planetary body in the scale language of Reltroner Studio.**

# 3. 🧭 Circumference and World-Map Scale

Using the canonical diameter of **54,646 km**, Asthortera's approximate equatorial circumference is:

```text
π × 54,646 km
≈ 171,675 km
```

The established Asthortera world map uses a horizontal planetary scale of approximately **171,688 km**.

The difference is negligible for worldbuilding purposes.

```text
Canonical Diameter
54,646 km
        ↓
Approximate Equatorial Circumference
171,675 km
        ↓
Asthortera World-Map Width
~171,688 km
```

This means the map is not merely aesthetically large. Its horizontal scale is effectively aligned with the circumference implied by the canonical planetary diameter.

# 4. 🌊 A Land-Rich Civilization World

Asthortera differs from Earth not only in total size but also in its land-water distribution.

## Asthortera

- **~48% land**
- **~52% water**
- **~4.503 billion km² of land**
- **~4.878 billion km² of water**

## Earth

- **~29% land**
- **~71% water**
- **~147.9 million km² of land**
- **~362.2 million km² of water**

Asthortera therefore contains approximately:

> **30.4 times Earth's total land surface.**

Its water surface alone is approximately:

> **13.5 times Earth's total water surface.**

This combination—extreme planetary size and unusually high land availability—is essential to Asthortera's civilizational structure.

It creates room for:

- continental-scale sovereign states;
- megacity networks;
- giant ecological reserves;
- mountain systems;
- mega-rivers and inland waters;
- civilization-specific biomes;
- aviation corridors;
- road civilizations;
- maritime systems;
- wilderness regions;
- agricultural zones;
- research territories;
- vertically developed metropolitan systems;
- subterranean infrastructure;
- and civilization-scale industrial zones.

Asthortera is therefore not crowded merely because trillions of sentient beings live there. Its physical stage is correspondingly enormous.

# 7. 👥 Population, Demography, and Migration

Asthortera's canonical sentient population is approximately:

> **4.412 trillion sentient beings**

Its demographic structure is strongly shaped by interstellar migration.

## 7.1 Population Composition

- **61%** — Humans of Depeisit planetary descent
- **24%** — Humans of Cistheta planetary descent
- **7%** — Native Asthorteran Human-Wizard-Witch populations
- **5%** — Cosmeilia-Human mixed populations
- **3%** — Other alien humanoids, hybrids, and recognized sentient groups

Most modern population growth is connected to migration from older, stagnant, unstable, or collapsing civilizations.

Asthortera has therefore become more than a homeworld. It is widely understood as a destination for **personal reinvention, meritocratic opportunity, and civilizational second chances**.

> **"In Asthortera, rebirth is not a myth—it is a migration."**

## 7.2 Long-Lived Population Structure

Asthortera's demographic profile reflects a mature and stable civilization:

- **Median age:** ~252 years
- **Life expectancy:** ~475 years
- **Urbanization rate:** ~89.5%
- **Age-pyramid tendency:** broad middle-age population, tapering gradually toward both younger and older groups

The long lifespan changes the meaning of education, career development, institutional memory, family history, political continuity, and long-term planning.

A citizen may participate in several major economic, technological, cultural, or professional eras within one lifetime.

# 15. 🌕 The Twelve-Moon Planetary Identity

Asthortera is orbited by **12 natural satellites**.

The established lunar system includes:

- **4 major moons**, described as Earth-sized;
- **8 minor moons**, functioning as smaller dwarf satellites;
- and a symbolic relationship between the twelve moons and the twelve-month calendar.

The existence of twelve moons does not mean all twelve must always be visible simultaneously.

The canonical principle is:

> **Twelve moons exist continuously. Twelve moons are not continuously visible.**

Depending on location and time, an observer may see:

- no visible moons;
- one dominant moon;
- several moons;
- a large moon with faint secondary satellites;
- or, during unusual alignments, a much larger simultaneous grouping.

Visibility depends on:

- orbital position;
- orbital inclination;
- local horizon geometry;
- illumination;
- lunar phase;
- atmospheric clarity;
- latitude;
- daylight conditions;
- apparent brightness;
- and temporary astronomical alignment.

The twelve moons therefore function as a **planetary identity anchor**, not a rigid visual requirement for every Asthorteran scene.

# 16. 🪐 Gravity and Biological Adaptation

Asthortera's current canonical surface gravity is approximately:

> **11 m/s²**

This is about **1.12 times Earth's surface gravity**.

Despite the stronger gravitational environment, Asthorteran humans and human-like populations maintain relatively tall average heights.

## Average Adult Height

| Population | Male | Female |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Asthortera** | **174.8 cm** | **162.4 cm** |
| **Earth benchmark** | **~171 cm** | **~158 cm** |

Asthorteran biological adaptation is explained through several interacting mechanisms.

## 16.1 Genetic Adaptation

Over long evolutionary and migratory periods, populations developed:

- stronger skeletal structures;
- denser musculature;
- improved cardiovascular efficiency;
- more resilient connective tissue;
- and body proportions adapted to elevated gravitational loading.

## 16.2 Advanced Medicine and Nutrition

High-SDI societies provide:

- strong childhood nutrition;
- mineral optimization;
- preventive medicine;
- skeletal monitoring;
- regenerative treatment;
- and long-term developmental healthcare.

These systems allow healthy vertical growth despite stronger gravity.

## 16.3 Selection and Biomechanical Adaptation

Natural selection and long-term population adaptation favor body structures capable of balancing:

- stability;
- strength;
- endurance;
- mobility;
- and vertical development.

## 16.4 Gravity-Resistant Biostructures

Asthorteran biology includes reinforced protein-fiber and tissue structures that reduce gravitational compression and support long-term musculoskeletal health.

The result is not immunity to gravity.

It is a civilization whose biology, medicine, infrastructure, and long-term adaptation have developed together under the planet's gravitational conditions.
